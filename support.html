  • Aboneliği iptal etmek: iPhone'da Ayarlar > [adın] > Abonelikler > LingoVe.

Support

Having a problem, or have a suggestion? Write to lingove0@gmail.com.

  • Cancelling your subscription: on your iPhone, Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > LingoVe.
  • Restoring a purchase: the "Restore purchases" button on the Premium screen in the app.
  • Adding a widget: touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap the + button in the top left, and choose LingoVe.
